Sinigang

Sinigang is a popular Filipino sour soup. It is commonly cooked with pork, vegetables, and tamarind to give it its delicious sour taste.

Ingredients

  • 500 grams pork
  • Tomatoes
  • Onion
  • Radish
  • Eggplant
  • String beans
  • Water spinach (kangkong)
  • Tamarind soup mix
  • Fish sauce
  • Water

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil water in a cooking pot.
  2. Add the pork and cook until tender.
  3. Add tomatoes and onion.
  4. Add the tamarind soup mix and stir well.
  5. Add radish, eggplant, and string beans.
  6. Cook until the vegetables are tender.
  7. Add kangkong and season with fish sauce.
  8. Simmer for a few more minutes.
  9. Serve hot with rice.
