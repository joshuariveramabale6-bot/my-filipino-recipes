Chicken Adobo

Chicken Adobo is a popular Filipino dish cooked with chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Garlic
  • Bay leaves
  • Black pepper

Cooking Instructions

  1. Wash and prepare the chicken.
  2. Mix chicken with soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and pepper.
  3. Let it simmer until the chicken is tender.
  4. Add bay leaves and cook until the sauce thickens.
  5. Serve hot with rice.
