Pancit Canton

Pancit Canton is a popular Filipino noodle dish made with stir-fried noodles, vegetables, meat, and savory seasonings. It is commonly served during celebrations and family gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 250 grams Pancit Canton noodles
  • Chicken or pork
  • Carrots
  • Cabbage
  • Green beans
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Soy sauce
  • Chicken broth
  • Cooking oil
  • Black pepper

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare and slice all the vegetables and meat.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pan.
  3. Saute garlic and onion until fragrant.
  4. Add the chicken or pork and cook thoroughly.
  5. Add the vegetables and stir-fry briefly.
  6. Pour in the chicken broth and soy sauce.
  7. Add the Pancit Canton noodles.
  8. Mix and cook until the noodles absorb the sauce.
  9. Season with black pepper and serve hot.
