  • Chicken Adobo

    • Chicken Adobo

    Photo: CapitalCRUZ, Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 3.0)

    A delicious Filipino chicken dish cooked with soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices.

    Ingredients

    • 1 kg chicken
    • 1/2 cup soy sauce
    • 1/2 cup vinegar
    • 5 cloves garlic
    • 2 bay leaves
    • 1 teaspoon black pepper
    • 1 cup water

    Cooking Instructions

    1. Combine chicken, soy sauce, garlic, and pepper.
    2. Marinate for 30 minutes.
    3. Put the chicken in a pot.
    4. Add vinegar, water, and bay leaves.
    5. Simmer for 30-40 minutes until the chicken is tender and fully cooked.
    6. Serve with hot rice. Enjoy!
  • Pancit Canton

    • Pancit Canton

    Pancit Canton is a popular Filipino noodle dish mixed with vegetables, meat, and sauce.

    Ingredients

    • 250 g Pancit Canton noodles
    • 100 g chicken
    • 1 carrot
    • 1 small cabbage
    • 1 onion
    • 3 cloves garlic
    • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
    • 2 cups water

    Cooking Instructions

    1. Heat oil in a pan.
    2. Saute garlic and onion.
    3. Add chicken and cook until done.
    4. Add carrots, cabbage, and soy sauce.
    5. Pour water and bring to a boil.
    6. Add noodles and cook until tender.
    7. Serve hot and enjoy!
  • Sinigang

Sinigang

Sinigang is a traditional Filipino sour soup made with meat, vegetables, and tamarind.

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork
  • 1 packet sinigang mix
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 onion
  • 1 radish
  • 1 bunch kangkong
  • 6 cups water
  • Fish sauce to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil water in a pot.
  2. Add pork and cook until tender.
  3. Add onion and tomato.
  4. Add radish and sinigang mix.
  5. Simmer until the vegetables are cooked.
  6. Add kangkong and fish sauce.
  7. Serve hot with rice. Enjoy!
